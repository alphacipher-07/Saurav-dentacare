About the Doctor

Care you can trust for every smile

Dr. Sourav Agarwal leads Agarwal Dentacare with a focus on clear communication, careful diagnosis and comfortable treatment. Whether you need routine care, orthodontic treatment or restorative work, the clinic is designed to make your visit straightforward and reassuring.
